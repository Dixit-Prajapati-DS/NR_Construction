Founder & Director

Narendra Prajapat

As the founder and driving force behind NR Construction & Consultancy, Narendra Prajapat brings over two decades of hands-on expertise in industrial and commercial construction. His philosophy is simple: every structure must be a testament to precision, strength, and integrity.
